About Camp-Assist

Smart College Complaint & Maintenance Management System

Camp-Assist is a web-based college complaint and maintenance management platform designed to simplify the reporting, assignment, tracking and resolution of campus maintenance issues.

Our Mission

To provide a transparent and organized platform where students and faculty can report maintenance problems and track their progress until resolution.

Key Features

  • Student and Faculty Registration and Login
  • Online Complaint Submission
  • Admin Complaint Management
  • Maintenance Staff Assignment
  • Complaint Status Tracking
  • Maintenance Work Remarks
  • Feedback and Rating System
  • Admin Dashboard and Visual Analytics

Technology Stack

Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript

Backend: Python and Flask

Database: MySQL

Data Visualization: Chart.js

How It Works

Report → Assign → Track → Resolve → Feedback
